> studying how brains process sensory information. my research focuses on the neural basis of olfactory navigation in *drosophila* — how flies smell their way through the world.

## what i'm working on

- 🔬 **phd research** — identifying neural circuits for olfactory motion detection in flies
- 🧪 **[emonet lab](https://emonet.biology.yale.edu)** — computational and experimental approaches to chemotaxis
- 🎓 **mentoring** — helping undergrads and high schoolers explore science
